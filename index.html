The professional standard

What “good” looks like

Story

Every second has a job

Hook → context → tension or value → payoff → next action. No random cuts just to look busy.

Craft

Clean before clever

Clear dialogue, intentional pacing, readable captions, consistent audio, motivated B-roll, restrained motion.

Business

Reliability sells

Understand the brief, hit deadlines, communicate clearly, show proof, follow up politely, track outcomes.

Rule: Finish and review the work before adding polish. A clean, persuasive edit beats a complicated edit that weakens the message.

Booking and discovery

Run a useful call, not a hard sell.

  1. Ask what content or offer they need to improve and why now.
  2. Ask who the audience is, what action they want viewers to take, and what success means.
  3. Confirm deliverables, aspect ratio, length, references, assets, deadline, revisions, and approval process.
  4. Discuss budget and scope honestly. Send a written proposal with timeline and payment terms.
  5. Close with a clear next step and date. Do not promise guaranteed views or sales.
Proof of skill

Build a portfolio that answers “can you do this?”

Create focused samples that match the job you want. Spec edits must be clearly labeled as practice and must not imply the brand hired you.

Sample 01

Talking-head authority clip

30–45 seconds. Clean dialogue, purposeful jump cuts, readable captions, 2–4 motivated B-roll inserts.

Sample 02

Story-led short

25–40 seconds. Hook, context, tension, payoff. Make the ending feel earned, not abruptly chopped.

Sample 03

VSL / direct-response ad

45–75 seconds. Problem, promise, proof, offer, objection handling, CTA. Keep claims substantiated.

Sample 04

Minimal-motion explainer

30–60 seconds. Restrained typography, clean hierarchy, simple callouts, consistent easing, no gratuitous animation.

Workflow practice

CapCut + Premiere Pro workflow

Use CapCut for fast short-form edits, captions, and quick versions. Use Premiere Pro when a project needs more detailed timeline control, audio work, organized media, or finishing. Choose the simplest tool that meets the brief.

Practice rule: CapCut is the fast default; Premiere Pro is the deeper editing option.
